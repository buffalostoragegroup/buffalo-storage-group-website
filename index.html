About Buffalo Storage Group

Rooted in the communities we serve

Buffalo Storage Group is the parent company behind a growing portfolio of storage locations in Western New York. Our goal is to give every property the support of a unified organization while preserving the local knowledge and service customers value.

“A connected group of local storage facilities, working together to serve Western New York.”

As the group grows, this website will make it easy to discover each location and connect directly with the facility that best fits your needs.
